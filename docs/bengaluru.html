Frequently asked questions

How many lines and stations does Namma Metro have?

Namma Metro has 4 lines running with 87 stations open over about 102 km (as of 2026-11-20). 3 more lines are under construction or planned. The longest running line is Purple Line, at about 44 km.

What are the interchange stations on Namma Metro?

You can change lines at 3 stations: Jayadeva Hospital (Yellow Line, Pink Line); Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station, Majestic (Purple Line, Green Line); Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road (Green Line, Yellow Line).

Which Namma Metro lines are under construction or planned?

Purple Line: about 1.3 km, expected around 2030; Pink Line: about 13 km, expected around 2027; Blue Line: about 66 km, expected around 2027; Orange Line: about 45 km, expected around 2030; Red Line: about 38 km, expected around 2031. Years are targets, not commitments.

Are the trains on this Namma Metro map live?

No. Trains are simulated from typical service frequencies so you can see how the network runs through the day. Track and stations come from OpenStreetMap; always check the operator, Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Ltd (BMRCL), for real timings.
