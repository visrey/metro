Frequently asked questions

How many lines and stations does Delhi Metro have?

Delhi Metro has 11 lines running with 274 stations open over about 413 km (as of 2026-11-21). 2 more lines are under construction or planned. The longest running line is Pink Line, at about 71 km.

What are the interchange stations on Delhi Metro?

You can change lines at 28 stations: Kashmere Gate (Red Line, Yellow Line, Violet Line); Anand Vihar (Blue Line, Pink Line); Azadpur (Yellow Line, Pink Line); Botanical Garden (Blue Line, Magenta Line); Central Secretariat (Yellow Line, Violet Line); Dilli Haat - INA (Yellow Line, Pink Line); Dwarka - Kakrola (Blue Line, Grey Line); Dwarka Sector 21 (Blue Line, Airport Express); Haiderpur Badli Mor (Yellow Line, Magenta Line); Hauz Khas (Yellow Line, Magenta Line); Inderlok (Red Line, Green Line); Janakpuri West (Blue Line, Magenta Line), and 16 more.

Which Delhi Metro lines are under construction or planned?

Red Line: about 22 km, expected around 2028; Blue Line: about 5.0 km, expected around 2030; Green Line: about 13 km, expected around 2029; Magenta Line: about 24 km, expected around 2027; Airport Express: about 12 km, expected around 2030; Aqua Line: about 43 km, expected around 2029; Golden Line: about 9.5 km, expected around 2027; Silver Line: about 7.4 km, expected around 2029. Years are targets, not commitments.

Are the trains on this Delhi Metro map live?

No. Trains are simulated from typical service frequencies so you can see how the network runs through the day. Track and stations come from OpenStreetMap; always check the operator, Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC), with Noida Metro (NMRC) and Rapid Metro Gurugram, for real timings.
