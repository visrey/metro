Frequently asked questions

How many lines and stations does Mumbai Metro have?

Mumbai Metro has 5 lines running with 86 stations open over about 97 km (as of 2026-11-21). 4 more lines are under construction or planned. The longest running line is Line 3 (Aqua Line), at about 33 km.

What are the interchange stations on Mumbai Metro?

You can change lines at 4 stations: Andheri (West) (Line 1, Line 2); Dahisar (East) (Line 2, Line 7); Gundavali (Line 1, Line 7); Marol Naka (Line 1, Line 3).

Which Mumbai Metro lines are under construction or planned?

Line 2 (Yellow Line): about 36 km, expected around 2027; Line 4 (Green Line): about 106 km, expected around 2027; Line 5 (Orange Line): about 25 km, expected around 2028; Line 6 (Pink Line): about 31 km, expected around 2027; Line 7 (Red Line): about 9.9 km, expected around 2027; Navi Mumbai Line 1: about 23 km, expected around 2028; Thane Ring Metro: about 28 km, expected around 2030. Years are targets, not commitments.

Are the trains on this Mumbai Metro map live?

No. Trains are simulated from typical service frequencies so you can see how the network runs through the day. Track and stations come from OpenStreetMap; always check the operator, MMRDA, MMRC (Line 3), MMOPL (Line 1) and CIDCO (Navi Mumbai), for real timings.
